##6. Quality, selection and sealed evaluation

Record per-point actual XYZ force/error and signed bias, RMS/peaks/saturation,
uncapped demand, work, wrist-relative-shoulder/torso, root/foot paths, actual
support, loaded-slip peak/p95, pelvis/torso/waist, actual q/dq/torque and q_PD
continuity. A target jump is not an equally large actual joint motion.

Separate startup, sustained movement, deceleration, connected hold and released
free recovery. After real release, do not score old anchor tracking; record natural
steps, residual speeds/displacement/support and unnecessary motion. Do not dilute
force metrics with inactive zero-force ticks.

M1 references remain: local4cm/4N, middle8cm/7N, long10cm/6.5N,
saturation15%, loaded slip.45m/s, yaw20°. They are references, not a complete
small-sample direction-rejection gate. Formal severe protections and Pareto
tolerances await the concrete pending table. No checkpoint selection on exposed
regression or sealed results. Independent evaluation must not alter live training
Task, physical prefix, any RNG or sampler state.
