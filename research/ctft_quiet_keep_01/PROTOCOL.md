Yi approved a matched test of adding B0 action retention only on physically verified quiet standing. Use one real historical U update block as the minimum experiment. Branch from original round 130048 (all parameters/Adam/replay/RNG) and regenerate its 1024 real online transitions once per arm, before either model updates. Each arm runs the original eight joint update groups. Compare SHA of real transitions and unmodified control actor with archived round 131072. Any unexplained difference invalidates the paired attribution; preserve details; do not substitute an unmatched control. This tests a local late-training intervention, not prevention throughout the original training.
Training reference: all original train-only native motion sources with a complete final second of actual constant standing z, zero force, both stable support and small physical motion. Use the prior quiet-source thresholds and conservative per-tick max-foot load of 30N. Report source count. Take four evenly distributed controls per qualified source during that second. Compute actor-visible fields from the saved physical state, public guidance=1, actual no-force history. Target is frozen B0 at the identical input and z, checked against recorded native q_PD. Fixed loss = mean squared joint target difference in rad / (0.10 rad)^2, added once to native actor loss at coefficient 1. Engineering choice, no scan. Only one joint Adam step per group. Matched uniform parent weighting; no external label for active/released rows. No extra transitions appended to replay and no changes to existing no-force episode proportions.
Fixed evaluation: same exposed quiet parent and matched no-force/24cm continue/12cm stop as previous study, same vertical softness. Archive can be reused for exact control actor after reproduction. Report no-force path/yaw, loaded-minus-unloaded forward motion, actual force/lag, stance slip, stop/release and hard boundaries. Descriptive directional improvement requires lower unloaded path and yaw with sustained contact increment at least 90% of control and contact error and force each within 10% of control, no new safety failure. Report actual effects even if the result is small. This cannot qualify generalization or establish a natural gait. No sealed, no sweep, no formal restart.
